# Backlog

Items deliberately out of scope for the demo, kept for later.

- **Editable JD after upload, with re-scoring.** Let recruiters edit the JD after it is frozen and re-rank candidates (async or live), propagating the change across all gates — not just Gate 1. Dropped for the demo; the JD is tuned once at upload and frozen (see ADR 0005).
- **Cross-candidate learning loop.** Outcomes / retention refine the scoring model over time (the green feedback loop, the compounding moat). Out of scope for the demo (see ADR 0004).
- **Outreach response capture.** Capturing a prospect's reply to outreach (email / LinkedIn). Out of scope for the demo (see ADR 0001).
- **Candidate reuse across multiple JDs.** One person as a single Candidate who can sit in several requisitions' pipelines. Out of scope for the demo, which runs a single JD (HR Assistant).
