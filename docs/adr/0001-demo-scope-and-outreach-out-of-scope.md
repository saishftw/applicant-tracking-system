# Demo scope: two-part flow; outreach-response capture out of scope

The demo is split into two parts. **Part 1** shows sourcing, scoring, and the Gate 1 shortlist + swipe on passive prospects (the "own the ranking" moat). **Part 2** shows gate progression across Gates 2–6 using pre-seeded candidates who have already responded.

Capturing a prospect's response to outreach (email / LinkedIn) is deliberately **out of scope**: Part 2 candidates are seeded as already-opted-in Applicants. This keeps the demo focused on its core narrative — how a candidate transitions across the gates after applying — rather than building an outreach-response capture loop.
